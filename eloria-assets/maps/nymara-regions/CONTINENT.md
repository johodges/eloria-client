# The continent layer

How the eleven Nymara exterior maps join up, and what was added so a player
crossing from one to the next reads it as one country rather than eleven
unrelated packages. Everything here is generated by the region builds from
three shared sources: `region-connections.json` (the graph),
`_toolkit/transitions.py` (the marches) and `_toolkit/loresites.py` (the
set pieces the NPC threads point at).

## The graph

`region-connections.json` (schema 2) lays the regions out as the concept
board does: Four Gates at the centre of the lake, Crownwater filling the lake
as the ferry hub, and a ring running clockwise from the north -
Mirrorhold, Amethyst Barrens, Sunmane Steppe, Verdant Stair, Ssarathi Ruins,
Manymouth Delta, Westhaven, Grey Moors, Amberwood - with the Whitehorn Range
beyond the ring to the north. Twenty-four links, each naming a portal id on
both ends and a type (`walk`, `causeway`, `ferry`). Sunmane's east gate is a
frontier and links nowhere.

`eloria-assets/tools/continent_portals.py` turns the graph plus the packages
into the server's portal table: one `portal` line per direction per link, and
one pair per door a region declares into its insides map. It refuses to write
if any end is missing or if a trigger tile is not walkable on the generated
server map.

## The marches

Every crossing on a toolkit region is a *march*: the last forty-odd metres of
road before the portal, where the neighbour's country starts early. The
march paints the neighbour's ground class into the terrain along a warped
falloff (never over roads, paving, water margins or the region's own built
ground), scatters a handful of the neighbour's growth - Whitehorn's dark
pines and snow boulders into Mirrorhold, Amethyst veins into Sunmane, reeds
and a beached boat where a ferry lands - and dresses the road with the
region's own furniture: a waystation twenty metres back (a lamp, cairn,
tuned shard, wind banner, bollard, stela, menhir or lantern snag, depending
on whose road it is), a march stone naming the neighbour, and a signpost.

Each march is a `transitions.Crossing` in the region's build script
(`CROSSINGS = [...]`), with `prepare()` before the region populates (so its
own trees stay off the station ground), `paint()` and `dress()` after. Ground
classes are allocated per region from the free class ids, so a march never
collides with a region's authored surfaces.

| region | crossings |
|---|---|
| whitehorn_range | south-gate (Mirrorhold), west-pass (Amberwood), east-pass (Amethyst) |
| mirrorhold | north-pass (Whitehorn) and east-road (Amethyst), both on the north rim's cols; west-gorge, south-road (Four Gates), lake-quay (Crownwater ferry) |
| amethyst_barrens | north-pass, west-road, south-road (Sunmane), east-shore (Crownwater ferry) |
| sunmane_steppe | west-landing (Four Gates causeway), north-track (Amethyst), south-track (Verdant Stair) |
| verdant_stair | east-pass-gate (Sunmane), west-quay-gate (Ssarathi) |
| ssarathi_ruins | north-stair (Four Gates), east-causeway, west-causeway (Manymouth), south-gate (Crownwater ferry) |
| manymouth_delta | north-landing (Crownwater ferry), east-landing, west-landing (Grey Moors), south-landing (Westhaven) |
| westhaven | north-road (Grey Moors), east-road (Manymouth), crownwater-berth, amber-berth (ferries) |
| grey_moors | west-waygate (Westhaven), east-waygate (Crownwater ferry), north-gate (Amberwood), south-gate (Manymouth) |
| amberwood | north-pass (Whitehorn), east-road (Mirrorhold), south-road (Grey Moors), harbour-quay (Westhaven ferry) |
| crownwater | four quays plus three second berths, one per ferry route |
| four_gates | the four city gates, declared in `four-gates/world.json` with their server tiles |

Mirrorhold is a bowl whose east side is peaks with no way through; the only
ground that reaches its rim is at two cols on the north side, so the road to
the Barrens leaves by the north-east col and the road to Whitehorn by the
north-west one. Whitehorn's own three passes are level cuts through its rim
along the roads that leave (`grade_path` at the height of the ground the road
comes from); the bowl-shaped notches tried first left craters nothing could
climb.

Sunmane is not a toolkit region; its three crossings are `march_gate` pieces
from its own kit (two menhirs flanking the road, the gap between them the
crossing tile), declared as portal interactives in `settlement.py`.

## Lore sites

`config/eloria/npc_dialogue.txt` runs twelve arguments through the NPCs, and
until now none of them had anything to point at. `_toolkit/loresites.py`
builds the places, and each region's `SITES = [...]` asks for one near the
NPC who talks about it. `prepare()` searches the flattest open ground within
36 m of the ask (never a road, never water, never a slope), levels a soft pad
and keeps the region's scatter off it; `dress()` places the piece and
records it as a `lore-site` landmark with its thread letter and a note.

| thread | site | region | what stands there |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | nine-watchers | Amberwood | nine lantern-niched stones facing east, a fire never let out |
| A | deep-grove-scar | Amberwood | a ring of burnt snags round a charcoal circle, one sapling |
| B | ringing-quarry | Mirrorhold | the quarry face, the half-drawn blank, the testing bell |
| C | eleven-measures | Amethyst Barrens | eleven brass-collared calibration stones in an arc |
| D | court-grating | Crownwater | the grating over the drowned court, thrown back |
| E | league-post-house | Westhaven | letter-rack, sealed crates, the post returned unopened |
| F | breached-barrows | Grey Moors | a forced barrow, spoil heap, blank return-field stakes |
| G | stelae-court | Manymouth Delta | the water lineage in two ranks, the tenth stone blank |
| H | fourth-well | Sunmane Steppe | a well filled to the coping under a cairn built badly on purpose |
| I | root-tally | Amberwood | tally bench and daybook-stump either side of the bee fence |
| J | moved-anchor | Verdant Stair | the stair's anchor stone on rollers, and its empty socket |
| K | snowline-stones | Whitehorn Range | ten stones up a slope with brass lines where the snow stood |

Thread L (the ninety candles) is told on the bootstrap maps and has no
Nymara site.

## Small rooms

Every combined insides map gained three rooms from `_toolkit/amberwood/smallrooms.py`
- a cave (with a den), a cottage and a shrine - each with a door on the
region above and a section on the insides map:

| region | cave | cottage | shrine |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amethyst Barrens | Sour Cut (crystal cave spider) | the counting house | the measure house |
| Grey Moors | the Fifth Chamber (moorland dire wolf) | the peat croft | the Warm Stone, with crypt |
| Westhaven | the salvage hole (bronze tide crab) | Gullscar farmhouse | the haven undercroft, with crypt |
| Verdant Stair | Anchor Hollow (moonshadow lynx) | the physick still | the Nine Lost |
| Manymouth Delta | the paddy sump (Manymouth crab) | the bund warden's house | the bund shrine |
| Whitehorn Range | the cascade cave (glacier crab) | the gate store | the snowline cell |
| Ssarathi Ruins | the water-gate undercut (saltmarsh crocodile) | the lineage house | the Tenth Mouth |
| Crownwater | the Grating (abyssal armoured fish) | the case room | the Eleventh Bell |

The Whitehorn insides map grew from 32 to 64 server tiles to hold its rooms;
its registry transform moved with it.

## Density

The three densest packages opened up: Verdant Stair's canopy spacing
8.6 -> 9.6 m with a tighter near tier, Amberwood's forest spacing 6.2 -> 6.8 m,
Ssarathi's rim jungle 8.5 -> 9.3 m. Instanced triangles, from each package's
`world.json` `performance` block:

| package | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| verdant_stair | 3.56 M | 2.81 M |
| amberwood | 3.06 M | 2.80 M |
| ssarathi_ruins | 2.08 M | 1.82 M |

The paths stay readable: the near tier of the jungle still closes over the
walkways, the forest still reads as forest from the road, and nothing that
was a landmark lost its screen.

## Sunmane

Three things in the steppe's walk grid stopped a player leaving the camp at
all: the gate lintels were stamped onto the ground plane (fixed in
`collision.py`, which now ignores geometry more than 2.1 m above a node's
seat), the great hall's plinth ran through the palisade and the north gate
opened into its back wall (the hall is 13 m across now and 1 m further in),
and a cart stood in the south gate with the crossroads well on the road
behind it (both moved). The archive barrow is a barrow again: the Ssarathi
Royal Archive is entered from the Ssarathi Ruins.

## Rebuilding and checking

```sh
cd eloria-assets/maps/nymara-regions/<region>/source && python build_<region>.py
cd eloria-assets/maps/nymara-regions/<region>/source && python build_interiors.py   # or build_insides.py
cd eloria-assets/maps/nymara-regions/<region>/source && python export_insides_collision.py
# server side
python tools/sync_authored_collision.py --client <eloria-assets/maps>
python tools/generate_nymara_maps.py <maps dir>
python <eloria-assets>/tools/continent_portals.py --server . --maps <maps dir> --apply
python tools/author_region_content.py all --maps <maps dir> --client <client repo> --apply
python tools/relocate_map_content.py --maps <maps dir> --apply
```

The region builds validate their glTF and report every lore site's resolved
ground in `buildNotes`; the portal tool fails rather than write a crossing
whose trigger a player cannot stand on.
