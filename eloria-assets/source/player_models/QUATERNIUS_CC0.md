# Quaternius source assets

The luminous male and female source meshes are derived from the free Standard
edition of **Universal Base Characters** by Quaternius. The luminous player
animation source is derived from the **Universal Animation Library** by
Quaternius. The buzzed, simple-parted, long, and buns hairstyle sources are
also taken from the Standard Universal Base Characters pack; their copied
reference textures are reduced to the 512-pixel resolution embedded by the
runtime asset builder.

The Glasswarden ship on a second, slimmer base body. It is not a new source
mesh: it is the same Standard Universal Base Characters geometry, with the same
topology, UVs and skin weights, re-proportioned by the radial field in
`BASE_BODIES` in `eloria-assets/tools/build_native_nymara_glbs.py`. It is a
derivative of the CC0 original and carries no other provenance.

## What does not come from these packs

The **Human** race does not. It is the control arm of an experiment in
authoring a playable race from scratch, and it shares no geometry with the
Quaternius source at all: no vertex, UV, weight or texel of it is sampled,
deformed or copied from `Universal Base Characters`, from any race GLB derived
from it, or from any wardrobe or accessory mesh. Its skeleton offsets, mesh,
unwrap, skin weights and every texture are generated by
`eloria-assets/tools/human_race.py`.

What it does share is the *animation interface*: the joint names, the joint
order, the parent of each joint, and the rest rotations the shared
`Universal Animation Library` clips write their absolute rotation tracks
against. Those four things are a contract about the clips rather than a
description of a body, and a race that changed them would simply not animate.
They are exported once, on their own, to
`eloria-assets/tools/human_rig_contract.json` by
`eloria-assets/tools/export_rig_contract.py`, and that file is the only thing
the Human builder reads from the existing library.
